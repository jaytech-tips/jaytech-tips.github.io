User Data Deletion

Last updated: May 22, 2026

JayTech Tips respects your privacy. If you wish to have your data deleted from our systems, you can request deletion through the following methods.

How to Request Deletion

Send us a message on Facebook and we will process your request within 30 days.

📩 Message us on Facebook:

facebook.com/JayTechTips

Include the email or name associated with your account so we can locate your data.

What Gets Deleted

Upon receiving your request, we will delete:

• Any personal information collected through page interactions
• Messages sent to our page via Facebook Messenger
• Any data associated with your Facebook user ID

Data That Cannot Be Deleted

Public interactions on our page (comments, likes, shares) cannot be deleted by us — these are controlled by Meta's platform. You can delete your own comments directly on Facebook.

Processing Time

We will process your deletion request within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation via Facebook Messenger once the deletion is complete.
